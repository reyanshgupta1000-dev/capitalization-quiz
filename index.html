Rules (Read Carefully)

1. Join a room using a code.

2. Answer capitalization questions correctly.

3. Each correct answer = 1 point.

4. Wrong answers = no points.

5. If you make a mistake, you do not lose points — you just don’t earn one.

6. The player with the highest score wins.

7. Game lasts about 10–20 minutes total.

What This Game Teaches

This game teaches capitalization rules in English grammar.

You learn to capitalize names, places, and the first word in sentences.

Reflection Letter

This game helps you practice capitalization, which is an important grammar skill. Capitalization makes writing clearer, more professional, and easier to understand.

It is important because without correct capitalization, writing can look confusing or incorrect. I used to forget capital letters often, but this game helps train your brain to notice them.

My advice is to slow down when writing and always check the first letter of sentences, names of people, places, and countries. One thing I wish I knew earlier is that small capitalization mistakes can make writing look less serious even if the ideas are good.
