Game Rules (English Assignment Requirement)

Grammar Skill: Capitalization

Players join a room and answer grammar questions.

Each correct answer = +1 point.

Wrong answers = 0 points (no penalty, just no score).

Game is won by the highest score at the end.

If you make a mistake, you do NOT lose points, but you miss scoring opportunities.

This game lasts about 10–20 minutes.

What You Are Learning

This game teaches capitalization rules in English writing.

You learn to capitalize:

- Names of people

- Places (cities, countries)

- The first word in a sentence

Reflection Letter (To Future Students)

This game is designed to help you practice capitalization, which is an important grammar skill in English. Capitalization helps writing look clear, correct, and professional.

I think this skill is important because without it, sentences can be confusing or look incorrect even if the idea is good. In school, writing with correct capitalization makes your work easier to understand and more professional.

My advice is to always slow down when writing and check the first letter of every sentence. Also remember to capitalize names of people, countries, cities, and important places. One thing I wish I knew earlier is that small capitalization mistakes can affect your grade even if everything else is correct.

Tip for mastering this skill: practice reading sentences and circling capital letters in your head before writing. The more you practice, the more automatic it becomes.
