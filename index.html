🔥 Capitalization Battle Pro

📘 English Assignment Requirements

Grammar Skill: Capitalization

What the game teaches:
This game teaches how to correctly use capital letters in English sentences, including names, places, and sentence beginnings.

Why this skill matters:
Capitalization is important because it makes writing clear, readable, and professional. Without it, sentences can be confusing or look incorrect even if the ideas are correct.

Learning goal:
To help students recognize and apply capitalization rules automatically while writing.

Rules of the game:

  • Players join using a room code
  • Host starts the game
  • Each correct answer = 1 point
  • Wrong answers = 0 points (no penalty)
  • Highest score at the end wins

What happens if you make a mistake?
You do not lose points, but you miss the chance to increase your score.

How to win:
The player with the highest total score after all 15 questions wins the game.

Time: 10–20 minutes

Practice: 15+ capitalization questions included

📝 Reflection Letter (To Future Students)

This game is designed to help you practice capitalization, which is an important grammar skill in English writing. It helps you understand when to use capital letters in sentences, names of people, and places.

I think this skill is important because writing without correct capitalization can look unprofessional or confusing, even if the ideas are correct. Using capital letters properly helps readers understand your writing more easily.

My advice is to always check the first word of every sentence and make sure names of people, cities, and countries are capitalized. One thing I wish I knew earlier is that small mistakes in capitalization can still affect your grade.

A good tip for mastering this skill is to practice while reading — try noticing capital letters in books or articles so it becomes natural when you write.
