📝 Reflection Letter

This game helps you practice capitalization, which is an important grammar skill in English writing. Capitalization makes writing clearer, more correct, and more professional.

This skill is important because without it, writing can be confusing even if the ideas are good. It helps readers understand where sentences begin and which words are important.

My advice is to always slow down and check the first letter of sentences, names of people, cities, and countries. One thing I wish I knew earlier is that small capitalization mistakes can still affect grades.

A good tip is to practice spotting capital letters while reading so it becomes automatic when writing.
